Contact RoleGlobe Jobs

We welcome corrections to job listings, source disputes, copyright requests and technical feedback.

Current reporting channel

Our verified public business email has not yet been configured. Until one is provided, technical and editorial reports may be submitted through the project's public GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account may be required.

How to report a listing problem

  1. Identify the RoleGlobe job page or listing title.
  2. Link to the employer's official careers page where possible.
  3. Explain whether the job is closed, incorrect, duplicated or uses an unauthorized source.
  4. Do not post applicant details, identity documents, emails, passwords, bank data or confidential messages in a public issue.

Advertising and privacy

For advertising behavior use the site's Ad preferences control. For provider-specific data rights, consult the advertising provider's privacy policy. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

Business correspondence

A dedicated public inbox for private business or privacy correspondence will be published here after verification. No private customer data should be submitted through public issue comments.
