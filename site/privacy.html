Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. RoleGlobe Jobs is a static global job discovery website. We do not require an account or collect job applications directly. Job application links take visitors to independent employer websites with their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

Saved job IDs are kept locally in browser storage, and your advertising opt-out or opt-in preference is remembered using first-party browser storage and a first-party cookie lasting up to 180 days (where supported). The cookie can also work across RoleGlobe deployment preview subdomains. You may clear local storage through your browser. Website hosting and security services may process ordinary technical logs, including IP address, request time, user agent and approximate location.

Third-party Adsterra advertisements

RoleGlobe may load Adsterra ad-unit pages automatically without an initial permission popup on its Cloudflare Pages website. These pages run within same-site frames that execute the supplied advertising scripts from an advertising delivery domain, currently bauval.org. Adsterra and downstream advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and secure advertising. We do not control all advertising partner data practices or the ads individually shown. Use the Ad preferences link in the footer to turn advertising off. Your opt-out is remembered and prevents ad scripts on later visits; turning ads off reloads the page to stop active sessions. Browser Global Privacy Control also prevents ad loading.

Job information is accessible without advertising. Adsterra and partners can use cookies or similar technologies, and prior opt-in may be required in certain countries. This basic opt-out control is not a certified consent-management platform; the publisher must implement an appropriate regional consent solution before serving personalized or otherwise consent-requiring ads in affected markets. Advertising content is provided by third parties; avoid clicking any advertisement you do not trust. For data rights requests concerning an advertiser, consult the advertiser’s own privacy notices.

Google AdSense

The site has requested review from Google AdSense and currently uses a Google ownership verification meta tag. Google AdSense advertising scripts are not enabled. If enabled later, this notice and any required consent platform will be updated accordingly.

Contact and changes

For privacy, listing or advertising concerns, refer to the site contact page. We may update this policy as advertising partners, site features or applicable requirements change.
