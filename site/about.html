About RoleGlobe Jobs

Helping people explore global career opportunities while keeping the original employer source at the center of every application.

RoleGlobe Jobs is an independent job discovery and career education project built for students, recent graduates, experienced professionals and people exploring IT and non-IT work across countries. Our goal is to make job searches easier to organize without disguising who really posted a vacancy.

When permitted sources can be checked, the website displays relevant employer-hosted opportunities and directs candidates to the employer's own application page. Our guides explain international resumes, remote-work restrictions, recruitment fraud and careful job verification. We work to keep the website straightforward on mobile devices as well as desktops.

Being an independent portal matters: RoleGlobe Jobs is not an employer, recruiter, immigration agency or guarantor of employment. Job availability, visa sponsorship, location restrictions and salary terms remain subject to each employer's official posting. We do not charge visitors to view job listings or apply.

We distinguish independently written educational content from advertisements and provide visible privacy information. When a feed cannot be checked or a listing is withdrawn, users should always rely on the company careers page rather than a cached summary.

Our editorial priorities

  • Clear employer/source attribution and working application destinations.
  • Useful, independently written guides rather than copied recruitment descriptions.
  • Transparent notices about stale listings and changing eligibility.
  • Separation of sponsored advertisements from job search controls.
  • A correction process for inaccurate or inappropriately reproduced information.

For information about these standards, see our editorial policy.
