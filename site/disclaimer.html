Independent Website Disclaimer

RoleGlobe Jobs is an independent informational job discovery website. It is not an employer or government-affiliated recruitment agency.

Information only

Job listings, career guides and recruitment tips are for general educational and informational purposes, not legal, financial, immigration or professional career advice. Employer policies and eligibility rules may change without warning.

No hiring or visa guarantee

We do not guarantee any interview, vacancy, salary, sponsorship, work authorization, selection or employment. The hiring company alone controls its application process, closing dates, decisions and employment conditions.

Verify with official sources

Before applying, check the employer's own careers portal and confirm the location, remote eligibility, work authorization rules, compensation details and contact addresses. Never assume a listing is currently open merely because it appears in search results.

Employment scams

Do not pay intermediaries for purported guaranteed offers. Be cautious of requests for OTPs, bank passwords or unusually sensitive identity information. Report suspected fraud through appropriate official channels.

External sites and advertising

We cannot control independently operated employer sites, advertising networks or external resources. Advertisements are displayed separately from independently sourced employment information and do not constitute our endorsement.

Corrections

Report apparent mistakes or infringement with a public source link via the Contact page. We may correct or remove inaccurate records after review.
